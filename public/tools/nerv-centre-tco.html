How to read this. Estimates for comparison only, not a quote. All five options price the same reference capability — the AI inference node(s), the operations/database node, and storage — so the software running on it is excluded everywhere equally. Hardware uses NERV list pricing; cloud and maintenance rates use published market figures (July 2026) and are editable above. FX rates dated 2026-04-01. GPU markets move; a live quote is the standard before any decision.

We lean the model toward cloud. The cloud figure excludes egress, storage growth, snapshots, support plans, and multi-region high availability — the lines that usually grow real bills. If cloud still costs more here, it costs more.

Leasing. NERV Centre HaaS is a 36-month term with support and maintenance bundled, hardware refreshed at renewal, arranged through established equipment-finance partners — your capability stays OpEx and off your balance sheet, and there is no capital approval to wait for.
